DEAD SIGNAL

An original zombie rail shooter that runs inside your browser's DevTools console.

Play in the console

  1. Open DevTools with F12 (or Ctrl+Shift+J / ⌘+⌥+J) and pick the Console tab. Docking it to the side works best.
  2. Press Play in DevTools, or paste the snippet into the console of any tab and press Enter.
  3. The page turns into your light-gun pad. Aim and click on it while the frames play in the console.

Controls

  • Aim with the mouse · Click or Space to shoot
  • R, right-click or mouse wheel to reload (6 rounds)
  • P pause · M mute · V switch between console and on-page view
  • Run DS.quit() in the console to stop

What's out there

WalkerSlow, 3 hits. Headshots count double.
RunnerFast and weaving from wave 2.
BloaterPop it early; it bursts and hurts the pack. Up close it hurts you.
BruteBoss every 5th wave, with its own health bar.
SurvivorsLet them cross for +250. Shooting one costs a heart.
CratesShoot for +1 health or 8 seconds of double damage.

Best in Chrome or Edge. Firefox may not draw images in the console, so press V for on-page view there. If frames pile up instead of replacing each other, turn off "Preserve log" in the console settings.
